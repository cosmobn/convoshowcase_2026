# ICT Career Pathways for Students — based on BIICF

> **Source:** Brunei ICT Industry Competency Framework — https://www.biicf.bn/ (extracted 13 Oct 2026)
> **Focus:** where a student can start, the career ladder in each ICT sub-sector, and what is needed to reach each step (qualification, experience, skills, certifications).

---

## 1. Read this first

### Qualification levels (BDQF)
Entry requirements in BIICF are written as **Brunei Darussalam Qualifications Framework (BDQF)** levels.

| BDQF Level | Typical qualification |
|---|---|
| 8 | Doctoral degree |
| 7 | Master's degree, Postgraduate Diploma / Certificate |
| 6 | **Bachelor's degree** |
| 5 | **HND**, Advanced Diploma, Foundation Degree, IBTE Diploma |
| 4 | A Level, IB Diploma, STPU, HNTec, BDTVEC Diploma |
| 3 | O Level (A–C), NTec, Skills Certificate, BTEC Level 2 Diploma |
| 2 | O Level (D–E), SC2, ISQ, Competency Proficiency Apprenticeship |
| 1 | BTEC Introductory Certificate, SQ1 |

### Skill levels used in BIICF
- **Technical skills:** 1 Follow → 2 Assist → 3 Apply → 4 Ensure → 5 Strategise
- **Soft skills:** Basic → Intermediate → Advanced → Expert

The soft skills assessed for every role are Analytical Thinking, Communication, Creativity & Innovation, Decision Making, Negotiation, People Management, Resilience, Results Orientation, Service Orientation, Team Work and Work Management. Entry roles expect mostly **Basic–Intermediate**, mid-career roles **Advanced**, and top roles **Advanced–Expert**.

### The general rule for climbing the ladder
Most senior roles give two routes in. A degree shortens each step:

| Stage | With BDQF 6 (Bachelor's) | With BDQF 5 (HND) |
|---|---|---|
| Senior specialist / manager roles | + certification + **~5 years** | + certification or portfolio + **~8 years** |
| C-suite (CIO, CTO, CISO, CMO, BI Director) | + certification + **10 years at senior level** | + certification + **12 years at senior level** |

## 2. Where can an HND (BDQF 5) graduate start?

| Sub-sector | Role | Can an HND graduate enter? |
|---|---|---|
| IT Services | IT Technician | ✅ Yes (needs BDQF 2–4) |
| IT Services | Project Coordinator | ✅ Yes (BDQF 5) |
| IT Services | Systems Engineer | ✅ With a relevant industry certification |
| IT Services | IT Administrator / Manager | ⚠️ With industry experience or a portfolio |
| Telecom & Network | Network Technician | ✅ Yes (needs BDQF 3–4) |
| Telecom & Network | Network Engineer (Specialist) | ✅ Yes (BDQF 5 in IT/Telecom) |
| Telecom & Network | Network Team Lead | ⚠️ With industry experience or a portfolio |
| Apps & Solutions Dev | Junior Programmer | ✅ With an industry certification **or** portfolio |
| ICT Security | Associate Security Analyst | ✅ Yes (BDQF 5) |
| Data & AI | Data Entry | ✅ Yes (needs BDQF 4) |
| Data & AI | Data Analyst | ⚠️ With industry experience or a portfolio |
| Digital Media | Market Analyst | ❌ Needs marketing/communications/economics study + 3–5 years |

**Tip for students:** a strong **portfolio** (projects, GitHub, internships) plus **one entry-level certification** opens most of the ⚠️ roles.

## 3. IT Services

```
IT Technician ─┬─► IT Administrator / Manager ─┐
               └─► Project Coordinator ─────────┴─► Systems Engineer ─► Project Manager ─► IT Operations Manager ─► Chief Information Officer (CIO)
```

| Step | Role | Qualification | Experience |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | IT Technician (Levels 1–3) | L1: BDQF 2 · L2: BDQF 3 · L3: BDQF 4 (or BDQF 3 + certification) | L1 needs industry experience |
| 2a | IT Administrator / Manager | BDQF 6, or BDQF 5 + experience/portfolio | Relevant experience |
| 2b | Project Coordinator | BDQF 5, or a project management certification | With experience if certification route |
| 3 | Systems Engineer | BDQF 6 (IT/CS/SE), or BDQF 5 + industry certification | — |
| 4 | Project Manager | BDQF 6 (Business IT/IS), or BDQF 5 + PM certification | A few years |
| 5 | IT Operations Manager | BDQF 6 + certification, or BDQF 5 + certification/portfolio | 5 yrs (L6) / 8 yrs (L5) |
| 6 | Chief Information Officer | BDQF 6 + certification, or BDQF 5 + certification | 10 yrs senior (L6) / 12 yrs senior (L5) |

**How to get there**

- **IT Technician**: help desk, hardware/software installation, basic networking, backups.
  - *Build:* Information Security Management and Software Testing (Level 2–3); basic application development, databases, infrastructure and networking (Level 1–2).
  - *Certify:* CompTIA A+, Network+, Security+, Cisco CCT, CCNA, Microsoft 365 Fundamentals.
- **IT Administrator / Manager**: runs IT operations, assets, budget and staff.
  - *Build (Level 3 → 4):* Business Analysis, Budgeting, IT Asset Management, Problem Management, Procurement, Service Level Management, IT Project Management.
  - *Certify:* ITIL, CompTIA Network+/Project+/Cloud Essentials, Microsoft Systems Administration, Linux/Red Hat Sysadmin, COBIT 5.
- **Project Coordinator**: supports the project manager with plans, resources and schedules.
  - *Build (Level 3):* IT Project Management, Business Analysis, Business Risk, Contract, Vendor and Procurement management. Communication and Team Work at Advanced.
  - *Certify:* CAPM, CompTIA Project+, PRINCE2 Foundation, APM PFQ, Microsoft Project.
- **Systems Engineer**: deploys and maintains servers, systems, cloud and vendors.
  - *Build:* System Integration and Vendor Management (Level 4); Cloud Computing, IT Architecture, Infrastructure Management and Information Security (Level 3).
  - *Certify:* CCNA, CompTIA Server+, ITIL 4 Foundation, MCSE, Azure Fundamentals, VMware VCP, Linux/Red Hat Sysadmin.
- **Project Manager**: delivers IT projects on time and on budget.
  - *Build (Level 4):* IT Project Management, Business Analysis, Business Risk, Contract, Vendor and Procurement management. All soft skills at Advanced.
  - *Certify:* PMP, PRINCE2, APM PMQ, Agile Project Management, PMI-RMP, PgMP.
- **IT Operations Manager**: SLAs, service delivery, DevOps, disaster recovery.
  - *Build (Level 4):* Business Continuity, Disaster Recovery, Database Administration, Data Centre Facilities, Service Level Management, Application Integration/Support.
  - *Certify:* ITIL, PMP, CISA, Certified ScrumMaster, Azure Administrator, AWS SysOps, VCP-DCV, RHCSA, Python/Bash.
- **CIO**: sets enterprise IT and digital strategy.
  - *Build (Level 5):* IT Strategy, IT Governance, Enterprise Architecture, Cloud, Cyber Risk, Infrastructure Strategy, Stakeholder and Vendor Management.
  - *Certify:* Certified CIO Programme, CGEIT, CRISC, CISM, CISSP, CITM.

## 4. Telecommunications and Network

```
Network Technician ─► Network Team Lead ─┬─► Network Engineer ────────┐
                                         └─► Infrastructure Engineer ─┴─► Infrastructure Manager ─► Chief Technology Officer (CTO)
```

| Step | Role | Qualification | Experience |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Network Technician | BDQF 4 (IT/Telecom), or BDQF 3 + certification | — |
| 2 | Network Team Lead | BDQF 6 (IS/CS), or BDQF 5 + experience/portfolio | Relevant experience |
| 3a | Network Engineer | Specialist: BDQF 5 (IT/Telecom) · Expert/Management: BDQF 5 + 3 yrs | 0 / 3 yrs |
| 3b | Infrastructure Engineer | BDQF 6 + certification, or BDQF 5 + certification/portfolio | 5 yrs (L6) / 8 yrs (L5) |
| 4 | Infrastructure Manager | BDQF 6 + certification, or BDQF 5 + certification/portfolio | 5 yrs (L6) / 8 yrs (L5) |
| 5 | Chief Technology Officer | BDQF 6 + certification, or BDQF 5 + certification | 10 yrs managerial (L6) / 12 yrs senior (L5) |

**How to get there**

- **Network Technician**: installs, cables, configures and troubleshoots networks.
  - *Build (Level 3):* Network Administration & Maintenance, Network Configuration, Infrastructure Support, Telecom Network Management.
  - *Certify:* CCNA, CCENT, BICSI Technician, Data Cabling Installer, Cisco Optical Specialist, CETma.
- **Network Team Lead**: leads the operations team, handles faults, runs 24/7 support.
  - *Build (Level 4):* Network Configuration, Fault Management, Network Security Management, Telecom Network Management, Emerging Technology.
  - *Certify:* CCNP/CCIE, Huawei HCNA/HCNE, CompTIA Network+, Juniper JNCIE-ENT, Wireshark WCNA, Certified Fibre Optic Technician.
- **Network Engineer**: designs LAN/WAN/wireless, monitors performance, network security.
  - *Build (Level 4 → 5):* Infrastructure Management, Telecom Network Management, Network Security, Business Analysis, Business Risk.
  - *Certify:* CCNA → CCNP → CCIE, JNCIE-ENT, HCNA, ITIL, AWS Advanced Networking.
- **Infrastructure Engineer**: data centre, server, cloud and on-premises infrastructure.
  - *Build (Level 4):* Cloud Computing, Disaster Recovery, Enterprise Architecture, Infrastructure Strategy and Support, Service Level Management.
  - *Certify:* Huawei HCIA (Datacom, WLAN, 5G, Transmission, Access), CWNP, AWS Solutions Architect Pro, Azure Administrator, VCP-DCV, CCDE, PMP.
- **Infrastructure Manager**: infrastructure strategy, governance, vendors and team.
  - *Build (Level 5):* Infrastructure Strategy, Enterprise Architecture, IT Governance, Cloud, Security Architecture, Business Risk.
  - *Certify:* PMP, Telecommunications Management Professional (TMP), Certified Telecommunications Analyst (CTA), AWS SA Pro, Azure Administrator.
- **CTO**: overall technology and telecom strategy.
  - *Build (Level 5):* IT Strategy, Enterprise Architecture, Solution Architecture, Change Management, Business Agility, Stakeholder and Vendor Management.
  - *Certify:* CTO certification programme, CITP, CISSP, CGEIT, TOGAF 9, ITIL 4, 5G Infrastructure & Operations, ISO 22301.

## 5. Applications and Solutions Development

```
Junior Programmer ─┬─► Applications Developer ─► Application System Engineer ─┐
                   └─► Solutions Architect ────► Software Architect ──────────┴─► Software Development Manager ─► Chief Technology Officer (CTO)
```

| Step | Role | Qualification | Experience |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Junior Programmer | BDQF 6 + certification, or BDQF 5 + certification **or** portfolio | — |
| 2a | Applications Developer | BDQF 6 + 3 yrs, or BDQF 5 + 5 yrs (or portfolio) | 3 yrs (L6) / 5 yrs (L5) |
| 2b | Solutions Architect | BDQF 6 (CS/IT/SE), or BDQF 5 + 6 yrs | 0 / 6 yrs |
| 3a | Application System Engineer | BDQF 6 + certification, or BDQF 5 + certification/portfolio | 5 yrs (L6) / 8 yrs (L5) |
| 3b | Software Architect | BDQF 6 + certification, or BDQF 5 + certification/portfolio | 5 yrs (L6) / 8 yrs (L5) |
| 4 | Software Development Manager | BDQF 6 + certification, or BDQF 5 + certification/portfolio | 5 yrs (L6) / 8 yrs (L5) |
| 5 | Chief Technology Officer | BDQF 6 + certification, or BDQF 5 + certification | 10 yrs senior (L6) / 12 yrs senior (L5) |

**How to get there**

- **Junior Programmer**: writes code to specification, testing, version control.
  - *Build (Level 3):* Application Development, Agile, Software Design, Software Testing, Data Design, UI Design, System Integration.
  - *Certify:* HTML/CSS/JavaScript for Web Developers, Oracle Certified Associate Java, Junior Full Stack Java Developer, AWS Developer Associate, Google Cloud Developer, CompTIA Security+, Certified ScrumMaster.
- **Applications Developer**: builds, tests and maintains applications and databases.
  - *Build (Level 3 → 4):* Application Development, UI Design, Application Integration and Support, Software Configuration, Security Architecture.
  - *Certify:* Associate Android Developer, Flutter, .NET Core / ASP.NET Core MVC, Node.js, Oracle DBA, Azure Fundamentals, Scrum Developer.
  - *Languages to learn:* JavaScript, Python, Java, SQL/NoSQL, C#, PHP, HTML/CSS (also listed: C++, Go, Rust, R, Ruby, Swift).
- **Application System Engineer**: installs and maintains systems, automation, backups, disaster recovery.
  - *Build (Level 4):* System Integration, IT Project Management, Vendor Management, Service Management.
  - *Certify:* ITIL 4, PMP, CSEP, CISSP, CompTIA Security+, CCNA.
- **Solutions Architect**: designs solution architecture and roadmaps.
  - *Build:* Software Design (Level 5); Application Development, IT Architecture, Security Architecture, Quality Standards (Level 4).
  - *Certify:* AWS Solutions Architect (Associate → Professional), Azure Solutions Architect, Google Cloud Architect, TOGAF, CISSP.
- **Software Architect**: technical standards, high-level design, mentoring developers.
  - *Build (Level 4):* Solution Architecture, Data Design, Infrastructure Design, Security Architecture, Embedded System Interface, Stakeholder Management.
  - *Certify:* iSAQB CPSA, TOGAF, AWS/Azure Solutions Architect, Certified Kubernetes Application Developer (CKAD), IEEE SEMC.
- **Software Development Manager**: leads the development team across the SDLC.
  - *Build (Level 4):* Application Development, Software Design, Software Testing, Database Administration, Solution Architecture, Project Management.
  - *Certify:* Certified ScrumMaster, Certified Scrum Product Owner, PMP, CSSLP, AWS DevOps Engineer Pro.
- **CTO**: technology strategy, DevSecOps, cloud, governance.
  - *Build (Level 5):* IT Strategy, Enterprise Architecture, System Integration, Partnership Management, Quality Standards.
  - *Certify:* CITP, ITIL 4 Strategic Leader, AWS SA Pro, CGEIT, CCISO, TOGAF 9, CISSP.

## 6. ICT Security

```
Associate Security Analyst ─► Cyber Risk Analyst ─► Security Engineer ─► Cyber Risk Manager ─► Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)
```

| Step | Role | Qualification | Experience |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Associate Security Analyst | BDQF 5 (IS/CS), or BDQF 4 + 5 yrs (or portfolio) | 0 (L5) / 5 yrs (L4) |
| 2 | Cyber Risk Analyst | BDQF 6, or BDQF 5 + 4 yrs | 0 / 4 yrs |
| 3 | Security Engineer | BDQF 6 (Cyber/IT Security/CS), or BDQF 5 + 4–6 yrs, or BDQF 4 + 6–8 yrs | as listed |
| 4 | Cyber Risk Manager | BDQF 6 + certification, or BDQF 5 + certification/portfolio | 5 yrs (L6) / 8 yrs (L5) |
| 5 | CISO | BDQF 6 + certification, or BDQF 5 + certification | 10 yrs senior (L6) / 12 yrs senior (L5) |

**How to get there**

- **Associate Security Analyst**: monitors security, responds to alerts, educates users.
  - *Build (Level 3):* Cyber Risk Management, Incident Management, Network Security Management, Security Administration, Security Governance and Planning.
  - *Certify:* CompTIA Security+, CEH, GSEC, SSCP, GCIH, CASP+, OSCP, Huawei Security.
- **Cyber Risk Analyst**: risk assessments, controls, mitigation.
  - *Build (Level 4):* Cyber Risk Management, Security Architecture, Security Governance, Security Implementation and Planning.
  - *Certify:* CompTIA CySA+/CASP+, CEH, CISSP, CISA, CISM, ISO 27001, CCNP Security, HCIE Security.
- **Security Engineer**: designs and implements secure architectures.
  - *Build:* Security Architecture, Network Security Management and Infrastructure Design (Level 5); the rest at Level 4.
  - *Certify:* CISSP, CEH, CISM, CISA, CCNP Security, OSCP, Cisco CyberOps, RHEL Administration, CREST.
- **Cyber Risk Manager**: security policy, governance, compliance, awareness programmes.
  - *Build (Level 4):* Audit & Compliance, Cyber Forensics, IT Governance, Security Education & Awareness, Security Programme Management.
  - *Certify:* CRISC, CISA, ISO 27005 Risk Manager, ISC2 CGRC, CompTIA CySA+, CGEIT.
- **CISO**: owns the organisation's security strategy.
  - *Build (Level 5):* Security Strategy, Threat Analysis & Defence, Threat Intelligence & Detection, Cyber Forensics, Business Continuity, Disaster Recovery.
  - *Certify:* CCISO, CISSP, CISM, CRISC, GIAC GSTRT, CGEIT, NIST CSF 2.0 Lead Implementer.

## 7. Digital Media

```
Market Analyst ─► Digital Marketing Executive ─┬─► Business Development Manager ─┐
                                               └─► Customer Experience Manager ──┴─► Marketing Manager ─► Chief Marketing Officer (CMO)
```

**Note for computing students:** the first two steps need a **marketing, communications or economics** background. From the manager level up, an **IS/Computer Science degree plus a marketing or creative multimedia certification or portfolio** is accepted.

| Step | Role | Qualification | Experience |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Market Analyst | BDQF 5 (Marketing/Comms/Economics) + 5 yrs, or BDQF 4 + 3 yrs | 3–5 yrs |
| 2 | Digital Marketing Executive | BDQF 5 (Marketing/Comms/Economics) | 5 yrs |
| 3a | Business Development Manager | BDQF 6 (Marketing/Business/Creative Multimedia) **or** BDQF 6 (IS/CS) + marketing certification/portfolio; or BDQF 5 + certification/portfolio | 5 yrs (L6) / 8 yrs (L5) |
| 3b | Customer Experience Manager | Same three routes as above | 5 yrs (L6) / 8 yrs (L5) |
| 4 | Marketing Manager | Same three routes as above | 5 yrs (L6) / 8 yrs (L5) |
| 5 | Chief Marketing Officer | BDQF 6 + certification, or BDQF 5 + certification | 10 yrs senior (L6) / 12 yrs senior (L5) |

**How to get there**

- **Market Analyst**: analyses campaign data, traffic, leads and conversions.
  - *Build (Level 4):* Market Research, Customer Behaviour and Intelligence Analysis, Data & Trend Analytics, Media & Platform Management.
  - *Certify:* Google Analytics, Google Digital Garage, Meta Blueprint, HubSpot (Inbound, Content, Email), Hootsuite, SEMrush SEO.
- **Digital Marketing Executive**: runs digital campaigns, SEO/SEM, website and social media.
  - *Build (Level 4):* the same skills as Market Analyst, plus Service Management.
  - *Certify:* Google Ads, Google Analytics IQ, Meta Certified Digital Marketing Associate, DMI, AMA Digital Marketing.
- **Business Development Manager**: finds new business, manages the CRM pipeline, closes deals.
  - *Build (Level 4):* Business Development, Sales Strategy, Business Negotiation, Contract and Partnership Management, Customer Experience Management.
  - *Certify:* CBDM, CDMP, Certified Sales Executive, Salesforce Sales Cloud Consultant, Zoho CRM.
- **Customer Experience Manager**: customer journey, onboarding, retention, CXM platforms.
  - *Build (Level 4):* Customer Experience Management, Account Management, Product Management, Data Analytics.
  - *Certify:* CCXP, CCSM, Salesforce Service Cloud, Adobe Experience Manager, Qualtrics.
- **Marketing Manager**: integrated marketing plans, campaigns, AI-powered marketing tools.
  - *Build (Level 3):* Marketing Strategy, Integrated Marketing, Campaign Management, Brand Management, Pricing Strategy, Digital Advertising Platforms.
  - *Certify:* AMA PCM, CMMP, CDMP, Google Ads, Meta, TikTok Academy, Google Digital Marketing & E-commerce Certificate.
- **CMO**: brand, marketing technology, crisis communications.
  - *Build (Level 5):* Marketing Strategy, Digital Analytics, Digital Marketing Tools, Business Development, Customer Experience Management.
  - *Certify:* CMMP, Chartered Marketer, CCMO, MBA (Marketing), Certified Agile Marketing Specialist.

## 8. Data and Artificial Intelligence

```
Data Entry ─► Data Analyst ─┬─► Data Engineer ───────────────► Data Architect ─┐
                            └─► Artificial Intelligence Engineer ─► Data Manager ─┴─► Business Intelligence Director ─► Data Scientist*
```
*\*This order is as shown on the BIICF site.*

| Step | Role | Qualification | Experience |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Data Entry | BDQF 4 (Data Analytics/IT/IS) | — |
| 2 | Data Analyst | BDQF 6 (IS/CS), or BDQF 5 + industry experience (or portfolio) | Relevant experience |
| 3a | Data Engineer | BDQF 6 (CS/IS/SE), or BDQF 5 + certification | 0 / 5 yrs |
| 3b | Artificial Intelligence Engineer | BDQF 6 (AI/CS) + certification, or BDQF 5 + certification/portfolio | 5 yrs (L6) / 8 yrs (L5) |
| 4a | Data Architect | *Page unavailable on the site (server error)* | — |
| 4b | Data Manager | BDQF 6 or 5 (IS/CS/Statistics/Maths/Actuarial) | 3 yrs as a Data Analyst or similar |
| 5 | Business Intelligence Director | BDQF 6 + certification, or BDQF 5 + certification | 10 yrs senior (L6) / 12 yrs senior (L5) |
| 6 | Data Scientist | BDQF 6 (IT/CS/Maths/Stats/SE), or BDQF 5 + certification | 6 yrs (L6) / 10 yrs in data science (L5) |

**How to get there**

- **Data Entry**: enters, verifies and backs up data.
  - *Build (Level 2):* Data Analytics, Data Ethics, Data Governance, Database Administration, Data Migration.
  - *Learn:* Microsoft Excel, Google Data Analytics, Power BI, Power Apps, basic coding.
- **Data Analyst**: cleans, analyses and visualises data; builds reports and dashboards.
  - *Build (Level 3 → 4):* Data Analytics, Data Cleansing, Data Visualisation, Data Engineering, Database Administration, Data Ethics.
  - *Certify:* Microsoft Certified Data Analyst Associate (Power BI), Tableau, IBM Data Science, Google Data Engineer, aCAP, SAS.
- **Data Engineer**: builds data pipelines (ETL), data stores and platforms; deploys ML models.
  - *Build (Level 3 → 4):* Data Engineering, Data Design, Data Governance, Computational Modelling, Data Migration, Emerging Technology.
  - *Certify:* Google Professional Data Engineer, Azure Data Engineer Associate, AWS Data Analytics Specialty, Cloudera CCP, IBM Data Engineer, TensorFlow Developer.
- **Artificial Intelligence Engineer**: builds, tests and deploys ML/NLP/AI systems.
  - *Build (Level 4):* Computational Modelling, Computer Vision, Intelligent Reasoning, Pattern Recognition Systems, Data Strategy, Test Planning.
  - *Certify:* Google Professional Data Engineer, AWS Data Analytics, IBM Big Data Architect, DASCA Big Data Engineer, Cloud Architect.
- **Data Manager**: data collection, surveys, market and customer insight reports.
  - *Build (Level 4):* Data & Trend Analytics, Customer Intelligence, Market Research, Quality Standards, Service Level Management.
  - *Certify:* DAMA CDMP, DGSP, Informatica MDM, Power BI, Tableau, GDPR Compliance.
- **Business Intelligence Director**: BI strategy, governance, dashboards.
  - *Build (Level 5):* Data Analytics, Data Governance, Data Visualisation, Data Warehouse, Data Security, Design Thinking.
  - *Certify:* TDWI CBIP, Google BI Certificate, Power BI Data Analyst, Tableau Certified Data Analyst, Qlik Sense.
- **Data Scientist**: advanced models, ML and deep learning, research with business impact.
  - *Build:* Business Analysis, Business Innovation and Emerging Technology (Level 5); Machine Learning, Data Modelling, Pattern Recognition, Intelligent Reasoning (Level 4).
  - *Learn:* statistics, Python/R/Java/C++, SQL/NoSQL, machine and deep learning, big data (Hadoop, Spark), visualisation (D3.js, HighCharts), model deployment, Agile, GDPR.

## 9. A student's action plan

1. **Pick a sub-sector** that fits your interests (sections 3–8).
2. **Check your starting role** in section 2 and confirm you meet its BDQF level.
3. **Get one entry-level certification** for that role, e.g. CompTIA A+/Security+, CCNA, Google Data Analytics, or a cloud fundamentals certificate.
4. **Build a portfolio**: projects, internships and GitHub. Several roles accept a portfolio in place of experience.
5. **Raise your skill levels step by step.** Each next role usually needs the same skills one level higher, plus soft skills moving from Intermediate to Advanced.
6. **Plan further study.** Moving from BDQF 5 to BDQF 6 cuts about 3 years off the experience needed for senior roles.

*All requirements are taken from biicf.bn job-role pages. Certification lists are the most relevant picks from each role's full list. See the role pages on the site for complete details.*
