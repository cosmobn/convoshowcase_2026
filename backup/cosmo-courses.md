# Our Courses

Our BTEC qualifications offer a wide range of practical, career-oriented options to create a diverse and engaging curriculum that motivates and inspires learners. With BTEC, learning partners have the flexibility to learn through real-life scenarios, embracing the concept of "learning by doing."

City & Guilds offers industry-recognised qualifications which are highly regarded by employers, focusing on practical skills and knowledge relevant to specific industries and job roles.

These courses are a blend of technical and soft skills, ensuring our learning partners become truly COSMO job-ready graduates. Our BTEC & City and Guilds programs are designed to provide learning partners with the knowledge and experience needed to excel in their chosen career path.

[Apply Now](https://ccct.edu.bn/applyonline/)

## Course Areas

### Business
**Awarding body:** Pearson BTEC

This program covers essential business principles for aspiring professionals in the fields of marketing, finance, and management. Gain practical skills through real-world projects and prepare for a successful career in the dynamic world of business.

[Explore Business](https://cosmo.edu.bn/business)

### Information Technology
**Awarding body:** Pearson BTEC

Learn about software development, network management, cybersecurity and more. This hands-on program equips you with the skills needed to thrive in the rapidly evolving IT industry, setting you up for various tech careers.

[Explore Information Technology](https://cosmo.edu.bn/information-technology)

### Creative Media
**Awarding body:** Pearson BTEC

Explore various media platforms, from film and TV to digital content creation. Through practical projects and industry insights, you will develop the skills needed to excel in the vibrant and fast-paced world of creative media.

[Explore Creative Media](https://cosmo.edu.bn/creative-media)

### Logistics Operations
**Awarding body:** City and Guilds

Learn about Supply Chain Management, Warehousing, Inventory Management and more! Get equipped with practical logistics industry skills and be ahead in this booming profession.

[Explore Logistics](https://cosmo.edu.bn/logistics)

### Occupational Health and Safety
**Awarding body:** City and Guilds

The Occupational Health and Safety qualification is designed for individuals who are responsible for health and safety within their workplace. It provides learning partners with the knowledge and skills to identify risks, implement safety procedures, and ensure compliance with health and safety legislation.

[Explore Health and Safety](https://cosmo.edu.bn/health-and-safety)
